Prime Minister's Disaster Relief Fund.

Devastating flash floods and catastrophic landslides have swept across parts of Nepal, leaving whole communities submerged and families displaced overnight. Torrential monsoon rains forced rivers past their breaking points, washing away homes, infrastructure, and livelihoods in moments. Search and rescue operations are working tirelessly around the clock to reach stranded survivors, but thousands of affected families are now facing an urgent crisis, left without safe shelter, clean drinking water, food, or access to essential medical care.

In moments of immense tragedy, direct action makes all the difference. You can extend a life-saving hand directly to those in need by donating to the Prime Minister's Disaster Relief Fund (PMDRF) through its official portal at the button below. Every contribution, regardless of size, goes directly toward immediate emergency relief, medical supplies, and long-term rehabilitation efforts for flood victims. Please consider donating today to help rebuild lives and bring hope back to the resilient communities of Nepal.
